# HR-M0005-01 — LibreOffice Rendered Artifact Quality

## Purpose

Human judgment is required because automated structural/runtime validation cannot establish whether the first real rendered YADG artifact remains visually and semantically usable after LibreOffice layout/finalization.

This review gates M0005 only.

## Required evidence

Implementation produces under:

```text
artifacts/review/evidence/M0005/
```

at least:

- `libreoffice-review.docx` — finalized DOCX from the representative synthetic M0005 scenario;
- `libreoffice-review.pdf` — PDF exported from the same refreshed LibreOffice document session;
- `review-manifest.md` — repository revision, LibreOffice version/platform/executable, input/output provenance, relevant validation command/result, and SHA-256 (or equivalent strong identity) for the review DOCX/PDF.

Evidence may remain generated/local; the durable review record must capture enough provenance to identify what was approved.

## Reviewer role

Project maintainer/user familiar with the intended template-first YADG output and able to judge the rendered artifact's practical document quality.

## Acceptance criteria

Approve only when the representative artifact demonstrates all of the following to the reviewer:

- overall page/document layout is coherent and usable;
- template-owned styles/presentation remain materially intact;
- headings, paragraphs, lists, generated tables, and figures are visibly present and not obviously corrupted;
- figure and table captions display sensible evaluated numbering;
- semantic references display matching target numbers rather than placeholders, `0`, broken-reference text, or visibly stale results;
- the numbered section reference is visibly plausible and matches the rendered numbered heading;
- the template-owned TOC/index evidence included in the scenario is visibly updated enough to be usable;
- no YADG control/front-matter/prototype text leaks into the final document;
- no obvious clipping, overlap, missing images, broken characters, or severe spacing/layout regression is present;
- the PDF corresponds materially to the finalized DOCX as viewed in LibreOffice.

This is not a claim of pixel identity with Microsoft Word and must not be judged against that nonexistent contract.

## Decisions

Allowed human decisions while M0005 is active:

- `approved` — satisfies the blocking gate;
- `changes-requested` — implementation corrects within milestone scope and regenerates evidence;
- `rejected` — blocks completion and requires explicit follow-up/planning as appropriate.

Waiver is not permitted.

## Gate

After the human decision is durably recorded, run:

```powershell
./eng/review-check.ps1 --milestone M0005
```

The milestone is not complete until this command succeeds together with all other M0005 completion obligations.
